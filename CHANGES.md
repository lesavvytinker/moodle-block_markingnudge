# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-28)
- **On course and category pages too.** On a course page it counts that course and links to its marking grid; on a
  course category page it counts the assessor's courses in that category and its sub-categories and links to them.
  The Dashboard still counts all their courses. Hidden from anyone who doesn't mark there.
- Editing teachers can add it (new installs; on existing sites allow *block/markingnudge:addinstance* for Teacher).
- "adjustments to confirm" is now "Moodle overrides to check".
- Needs Evidence Reporter 0.35.0.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-27)
- New count: **RA/LLN to record** (learners and assessments where reasonable adjustment and LLN aren't recorded yet),
  shown in red. Needs Evidence Reporter 0.29.0.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-26)
- First version: totals across the assessor's courses from Evidence Reporter's marking page, colour-coded for waiting
  past the amber and red thresholds, with a button to the marking page.
